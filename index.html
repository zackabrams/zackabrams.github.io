Latest feature

Buying memecoins with credit cards on Robinhood Wallet and Fomo appears to sidestep card-network crypto rules

Test purchases of memecoins made with Visa and Mastercard credit cards were coded as “digital goods media” — the category that covers audiobooks and digital movies — and earned ordinary card rewards.

  • Purchases routed through Crossmint’s token checkout cleared via Apple Pay and Google Pay with no separate ID check.
  • The Block’s test transactions were classified as “digital goods media” and earned points or cash back.
  • Chase said the transaction should not have earned rewards and opened a case with Visa.
  • The New York State Attorney General’s office said it is reviewing the payment structure.
