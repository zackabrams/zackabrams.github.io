HOW TO PLAY

A fair exchange.

  1. Pick one word from each column. Tap anywhere on a word to select it. Drag a handle to move it up or down; words stay on their own side. Choose “Reorder” to show optional move buttons, or focus a handle and use the arrow keys.
  2. Swap exactly one letter from each. All other letters stay put.
  3. Find four answers in the category shown above the board: Compound words (easy: two meaningful words joined into one), Names (medium: proper nouns such as people, places, and story titles), or Hidden words (hard: an ordinary single word revealed by joining the new pieces, like FOR + GET → FORGET).

The swapped letters can be anywhere—even in different positions. Read the result in either order, with or without a space. Every letter in the answer comes from your two cards; no extra words are needed.

Colors stay on the original letter tiles, even after you reorder the words. They never reveal the replacement letter. The hint button colors one random, unrevealed tile.

Use all eight cards to find four answers. Press Start to reveal the board. The timer runs until your last answer, including time away from the game; there is no time limit. Progress and results are saved on this browser. Finish a puzzle on consecutive days to build a play streak; multiple wins in one day count once.
